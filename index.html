Dr. Saeed Vahidian is an Assistant Professor in the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Oklahoma State University. Before joining OSU, he completed his postdoctoral research at Duke University, where he worked with Prof. Yiran Chen, Director of the NSF AI Institute for Edge Computing (Athena).

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of California San Diego under the supervision of Prof. Bill Lin.

His research lies at the intersection of multimodal learning, large language models, generative AI, agentic AI, synthetic data, and robust learning. He develops reliable and efficient learning systems that connect foundational AI research with real-world applications.

Research Vision

My group develops principled and practical AI methods that learn from multiple modalities, generate useful data, remain robust under distribution shifts, and act reliably in complex environments.

Multimodal Learning and Vision–Language Models

Building models that integrate vision, language, audio, and structured signals, with an emphasis on cross-modal reasoning, alignment, data efficiency, and generalization.

Large Language Models and Generative AI

Developing efficient and reliable foundation models, controllable generative systems, multimodal synthetic-data pipelines, and methods that improve how large models learn and adapt.

Agentic AI and Autonomous Intelligent Systems

Designing AI agents that reason, plan, use tools, collaborate, and learn from feedback while remaining dependable, interpretable, and aligned with their intended objectives.

Robust, Efficient, and Data-Centric Learning

Studying robust optimization, dataset distillation, synthetic data, federated learning, and efficient inference under limited data, compute, communication, and privacy budgets.
